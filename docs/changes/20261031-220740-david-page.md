# David Page

Ported the `david.html` meme page from v1 (`AardeYamz/v1.yannislam.org`) to a
new `/david` route.

- `DavidComponent` (`components/other/david/`) renders a title, subtitle and
  an image grid. The copy and image list are in `config.json` under
  `about.david`, typed by `DavidPage` in `site-config.model.ts`.
- The four photos were resized from the v1 originals (one was 11.5 MB) to
  1600px JPEGs under `src/assets/images/david/`.
- Like `/aardeyamz` it is not in the nav, but it is prerendered
  (`app.routes.server.ts`), covered by the cache-header rule in `vercel.json`
  and by the smoke, asset and navigation e2e tests. It is left out of
  `sitemap.xml` on purpose.
- Dropped from v1: the links on the images to David's Facebook profile and
  `mailto:` address (a third party's contact details on a public page) and the
  link to the v1 Henry page, which has no v3 equivalent.
